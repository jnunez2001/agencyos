AgencyOS

Privacy policy

AgencyOS is an operating system for digital agencies: clients, projects, tasks, meetings, time, SOPs and client reports in one place. Each agency's information is kept separate from every other agency's. This page says what it does with information, including data from Google.

What AgencyOS keeps

Accounts of the people who use it (name, username, role, and a Google email when someone signs in with Google), and the agency's own work: clients, projects, tasks, notes, reports and results. It is stored on a private server.

Signing in with Google

When someone signs in with Google, AgencyOS asks Google for their name and email address only. It uses them to recognize an account that an administrator invited. AgencyOS never creates an account for a stranger.

Google Search Console and Google Analytics

When an administrator connects a Google account, AgencyOS asks for read-only access to Search Console and Google Analytics. It reads monthly totals (such as clicks, impressions, average position, sessions, users and conversions) for the websites the administrator chooses, and shows them in the agency's own reports. It cannot change anything in Google.

The access token is stored encrypted on the server. The data is used only to show results and reports to the agency's team. It is not sold, shared with other companies, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AgencyOS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Search Console and Google Analytics is used only to show results and reports inside the agency that connected it. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never used to train AI models, and no person at AgencyOS reads it except as needed for support the agency asks for, to keep the service secure, or to follow the law.

AI assistants

People in the agency may connect an AI assistant to their own account. It sees only what that person is allowed to see, and changes wait for approval by default.

Removing access and data

An administrator can remove a connected Google account in Settings at any time, which also asks Google to revoke the access. You can also remove AgencyOS from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for your information to be deleted, write to the contact below.
