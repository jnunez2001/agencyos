AgencyOS

The operating system for agencies

AgencyOS puts everything an agency runs on in one place: clients and their goals, projects and tasks, a shared calendar, meeting notes that turn into decisions, requests and follow-ups, time tracking and retainer hours, standard operating procedures with quality checks, and client reports.

How it uses Google

An agency can connect its Google account so that AgencyOS can read monthly numbers from Google Search Console and Google Analytics for the websites it manages. AgencyOS asks for read-only access, shows the numbers on the client's results page and in reports, and cannot change anything in Google. People can also sign in with Google instead of a password.

AgencyOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full privacy policy and terms of service.
